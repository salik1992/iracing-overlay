Inspired · double strip

Crescent

Only the outer arc of a 16×16 grid. This is the thin strip from the split digiflag, bent into the corner. The middle of the quarter stays empty.

Original · size

Bloom

A 12×12 quarter where the dots grow as they leave the corner. The flag reads largest toward the middle of the screen.

Original · Bloom, sizes reversed

Ebb

The same 12×12 quarter as Bloom. The dots that were smallest are now largest, so the corner carries the flag and the outer arc thins out.

Cove

Reversed sizes again, and the arc bowed the other way. The curve scoops in toward the corner instead of bulging out into the screen.

Original · two arcs

Twin

Two arcs with a gap. The outer arc is the flag. The inner arc adds a second cue: white inside blue, red inside yellow and debris, black inside red and the checker.
